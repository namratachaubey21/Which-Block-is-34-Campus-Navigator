Architectural Guide: Why are Blocks 33–38 Merged?

At Lovely Professional University, blocks 33 through 38 are built as an interconnected continuous mega-structure so students can travel between classes without stepping into extreme weather. However, for new students, this creates extreme confusion:

34

Which Block is 34?

Block 34 is the second wing in the spine. It connects directly to Block 33 on the West and Block 35 on the East. It houses the School of Computer Science & Engineering. To reach classrooms 34-501 to 34-512, enter via Block 33 atrium and take Lift Bank B straight to the 5th floor.

36

How to Navigate Block 36?

Block 36 contains the university's largest tiered lecture halls. It is located past Block 35. You do not need to walk outside; stay on Floor 1, 2, or 3 of the central indoor spine and walk East across the continuous glass bridge.

37

Why is Block 37 so Hard to Find?

Unlike other blocks that line the main southern boulevard, Block 37 sits perpendicularly on the North side behind Blocks 35 and 36! If walking outside along the main road, it is completely hidden. You must enter Block 35 and turn down the North breezeway beside the cafeteria to enter Block 37.
